## Description

A fixture page. It links back to [the list](jdocmanual?article=help/articles/articles#column-headers),
to [a page in another manual](jdocmanual?article=user/articles/adding-an-image-to-an-article),
and to [a page that was never imported](jdocmanual?article=help/nowhere/missing-page).

| Field | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Title | The title |
